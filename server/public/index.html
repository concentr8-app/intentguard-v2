Concentr8 Questions

The hard part is starting. We call you for that.

Every morning Concentr8 phones you, asks which task you will start and when, and then keeps distractions off your Mac until you have begun. Made for Berkeley students with ADHD.

Download for Mac Intel Mac

You decide the task and the time out loud. That is the part that is usually missing at 9 a.m.

  • 8:00

    The call

    It reads your upcoming bCourses deadlines and asks one question: which task, and when? You answer in a sentence. The call takes under a minute.

  • 9:30

    You start

    The app knows your task and your start time, and your session begins. You can also start any task by typing it in, even one that is not in bCourses.

  • 9:41

    You drift to YouTube

    A visible warning appears and you get a short grace period. If you stay, the app moves you back to your work instead of just scolding you.

  • 11:00

    You take a break on purpose

    Breaks are allowed. Pause the nudges, end the break when you are ready, and monitoring picks up again.

  • Later

    You see what happened

    Each session keeps a plain log of the nudges and returns, so you can look back without guessing.

What is inside

bCourses, connected

Paste your bCourses access token once, or use your calendar feed link if you do not have the token button. Assignments, quizzes and discussions come in on their own.

A call that asks, not tells

A calm voice, a few short sentences, one question. Say a time or say now. Say stop and it will not call again.

Task-aware focus

The app compares what you are doing with the task you chose. Your course pages and the work itself count as focus. A blank tab does not count against you.

Nudges that move you back

Known distraction sites and apps get a warning first. If you stay, the app returns you to where you were working.

Your phone number, your choice

You pick the call time. You can delete your account from the app at any time, and the call stops.

Simple to run

Download, drag into Applications, enter your phone number. No terminal, no setup scripts.

Questions

macOS says the app is damaged. What do I do?

The app is not signed by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it after the download. It is not damaged. Open Terminal (press cmd and space, type Terminal) and run this once:

xattr -cr /Applications/Concentr8.app

Then open Concentr8 from Applications as usual. If macOS only asks whether you want to open it, choose Open.

What does the app need permission for?

Concentr8 asks macOS for Automation and Accessibility access so it can see which app and page you are on and move you back to your work. It asks when it needs them, and you can change this in System Settings.

What do you store about me?

Your bCourses token stays on your Mac. The call server keeps your phone number, your call time and the titles and due dates of your assignments, so the call can name them. Delete your account in the app and this data is removed.

Which phone numbers work?

US numbers only for now. Enter yours with the country code, like +1 415 555 1234.

What if I do not want the call anymore?

Say "stop calling me" during the call, or turn it off or delete your account in the app.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and Intel Macs. It does not run on Windows or iPhone.
