Concentr8

Starting is the hard part. Concentr8 calls you in the morning, asks which task you start and when, then keeps distractions away. Made for Berkeley students with ADHD.

Download for Mac Intel Mac
  1. Open the download and drag Concentr8 into Applications.
  2. First start: right-click Concentr8, choose Open, then Open again (the app is not signed yet).
  3. Settings: enter your phone number and the invite code, tick the box. Done.
Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) is the first button. Not sure? Apple menu, About This Mac.
